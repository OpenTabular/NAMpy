bccg_lss
========

.. math::

   f(y)=\frac{(y/\mu)^\nu\varphi(z)}{\sigma y\Phi(1/(\sigma|\nu|))},\quad z=\frac{(y/\mu)^\nu-1}{\nu\sigma}

* **Natural domains:** :math:`y,\mu,\sigma>0,\quad\nu\in\mathbb R;\quad\nu=0:\ \log Y\sim N(\log\mu,\sigma^2)`
* **Conditional mean:** :math:`E[Y]=M_1,\quad M_k=\mu^k E[U^{k/\nu}\mid U>0],\quad U\sim N(1,\sigma^2\nu^2)\quad(\nu\ne0)`
* **Conditional variance:** :math:`\operatorname{Var}(Y)=M_2-M_1^2\text{ where finite};\quad\nu<0:\ M_k<\infty\iff\nu<-k`

Natural columns are ``mu, sigma, nu`` with log/log/identity predictors. Mu is not generally the response mean or exact median. Positive responses require a Jacobian and a truncated-normal normalizer. At nu=0 the law is lognormal. Mean is infinite for -1<=nu<0; variance is NaN there and infinite for -2<=nu<-1. Finite moment quadrature has explicit numerical limits, including negative-power variance below sigma=sqrt(machine epsilon). At least three distinct positive-weight responses are required; this necessary guard does not establish estimability of arbitrary formulas.

Distribution reference: `gamlss.dist::BCCG <https://github.com/cran/gamlss.dist/blob/master/man/BCCG.Rd>`_. Fitting uses NAMpy's joint penalized engine, not gamlss RS backfitting. See :doc:`/development/neighbor_parity` for the tested mapping and numerical comparisons.

Fixed smoothing and automatic joint REML with outer_newton, bfgs and efs are validated, including three smoothed predictors. Analytic likelihood derivatives through order four include the truncated-normal normalizer and the zero-power limit. Outer gradients/Hessians are checked with offsets and nonunit/zero weights; selected endpoints are compared to an independent derivative-free REML profile. Other method labels retain mgcv general-family coercion to REML, not separate ML/GCV/UBRE/NCV criteria. The optim route lacks family-defined null deviance scaling; magic and nlm.fd are also unavailable. Neural fitting, alternate links, censoring/truncation and deviance residuals remain unsupported. Generic conditional and approximate smoothing-uncertainty covariance transport are available; simulation/refit centile bands are not. Tight stopping rules may report nonconvergence at a float64 plateau; such fits remain ineligible for family ranking. This is distinct from mgcv's existing censored bcg likelihood.

Box-Cox Cole-Green regression models positive responses with three
predictors for mu, sigma and a real transformation power nu. Mu is
generally neither the response mean nor its exact median.

.. math::

   \eta_\mu=\log\mu,\quad\eta_\sigma=\log\sigma,\quad\eta_\nu=\nu.

The response Jacobian and positive-support normalizer are both
part of the likelihood. At nu=0 the response is lognormal; analytic
removable limits retain its power score and mixed curvature. Fixed
smoothing uses the existing joint mgcv-style solver with observed
second derivatives and exact higher derivatives for validated joint REML. Neural fitting uses the normalized density. Negative powers can give divergent response moments;
natural-parameter standard errors do not assert that response moments
exist. This family is distinct from the censored mgcv bcg family.

See :doc:`/statistical_reference/families/theory` for likelihoods,
links, weights and distributional predictors. The mathematical law
does not imply support for every fitting or smoothing-selection route;
the contracts below define the implemented combinations.

Target encoding, response/link column meanings and supported fitting criteria are specified in the tables and examples below. Scalar uncensored targets have shape ``(n,)``. Residual choices are family-specific; use :doc:`/statistical_reference/methods/residuals`. Log likelihood is higher-is-better; AIC/BIC are lower-is-better and require a normalized comparable likelihood.

Implementation definitions: :mod:`nampy.gam.families`. See the source link on the runtime contract for the implemented transforms and objective.

Types, defaults and output shapes
---------------------------------

.. autoclass:: nampy.gam.families.gamlss.bccg.BCCGLikelihood

Pass ``GAMLSS(family='bccg_lss')``; this family has no constructor options.

``predict(X)`` returns natural parameters as a float ``(n,3)`` array, ``predict(X, raw=True)`` returns link predictors ``(n,3)``, ``predict_point(X)`` returns the conditional mean ``(n,)``, and ``predict_components(X)`` returns an AdditivePrediction whose link and each term are ``(n,3)``. ``X`` is a DataFrame or numeric ``(n,d)`` array, consistent with formula mode.

Likelihood derivatives through order four are analytic, including mixed derivatives. Fixed smoothing is tested; automatic smoothing requires an enabled runtime capability and route-specific evidence. Residual availability follows the observation contract; ordinary response/Pearson residuals are unavailable for censored targets. Deviance residuals and null deviance are not defined by this implementation; use explicit response/Pearson diagnostics or shared distribution quantile residuals rather than the default deviance-residual plotting workflow.
