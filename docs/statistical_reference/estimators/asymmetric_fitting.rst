Expectile and quantile fitting conventions
==========================================

Both estimators compile an identified design and minimize a sum of weighted observation losses plus ``beta.T @ S @ beta / 2``. Fixed penalty strengths are supplied by the caller; scaling all weights changes effective regularization. Validation score instead normalizes by the sum of weights and excludes the penalty.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :class: api-reference-table

   * - Route
     - Training loss
     - Stopping and diagnostics
   * - ExpectileGAM: LAWS
     - Asymmetric squared error, weighted by level above zero and 1-level below zero.
     - Backtracked weighted least-squares updates; infinity-norm gradient tolerance scaled by 1+norm(X.T@(w*y), infinity).
   * - QuantileGAM: bandwidth=0
     - Exact convex pinball loss.
     - Sparse epigraph LP/QP. tol governs primal/dual accuracy; optimality records the maximum KKT feasibility, stationarity and complementarity residual.
   * - QuantileGAM: bandwidth>0
     - Smooth ELF: (level-1)*r + bandwidth*log(1+exp(r/bandwidth)), learning rate fixed at one.
     - Gradient-based smooth optimization; inspect the optimizer convergence flag and gradient diagnostic. Validation score remains exact pinball loss.

Separate levels can cross. Smoothing is fixed or chosen through validation. These APIs do not implement qgam learning-rate calibration or calibrated uncertainty. Upstream comparison scope is documented in :doc:`/development/neighbor_parity`.
