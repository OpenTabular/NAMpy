Calibrate future event-time lower bounds
========================================

Conformal lower bounds describe a future event time. Curve confidence
intervals instead describe uncertainty in a fitted function.

Conformal event-time lower bounds
---------------------------------

See :doc:`calibration_survival` for conservative observed-time and administrative-cutoff
survival conformal methods shared across both backends. They return one-sided
future-event-time bounds. They do not calibrate survival curves, confidence
bands or event-time upper limits. Delayed-entry conformal and automatic
censoring-mechanism estimation remain unsupported.

For methodological citations and paper links, see :doc:`/references`.
