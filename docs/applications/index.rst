Worked questions
================

Each page reads one tutorial fit as a modeling question, with the figure. The series are simulated, and the page says how they were generated.

* :doc:`Claim counts <claim_counts>` — exposure and extra-Poisson variation
* :doc:`Energy demand <energy_demand>` — temperature and time of day
* :doc:`Monotone risk <monotone_risk>` — a shape constraint in the basis
* :doc:`Housing-style effects <housing_effects>` — where a curve saturates
* :doc:`Positive losses <loss_distribution>` — a mean and a coefficient of variation
* :doc:`Time to churn <retention>` — censored time to an event
* :doc:`Regional measurements <spatial_pollution>` — neighbors on a graph
* :doc:`Quantile forecasts <quantile_demand>` — a quantile instead of one forecast
